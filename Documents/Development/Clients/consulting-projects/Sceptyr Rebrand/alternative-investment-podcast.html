Episode 47: Unlocking Alpha Through Alternative Investments

Former Goldman Sachs MD Marcus Thompson reveals how ultra-high-net-worth families achieve 15%+ annual returns through private markets

January 8, 2025 • 58 minutes • Hosted by Josh Tolley

About Our Guest: Marcus Thompson

Marcus Thompson spent 15 years at Goldman Sachs, where he rose to Managing Director overseeing alternative investment strategies for the firm's ultra-high-net-worth clients. He now manages a $2.3 billion family office fund and serves as Senior Investment Advisor to three billionaire families. Marcus holds an MBA from Wharton and is a CFA charterholder.

Episode Overview

In this revealing conversation, Marcus pulls back the curtain on the investment strategies that institutional investors and billionaire families use to consistently outperform public markets. This isn't about picking stocks or timing markets—it's about accessing entirely different asset classes that most investors never even know exist.

Marcus discusses the specific allocation frameworks, due diligence processes, and risk management techniques that have allowed his clients to achieve double-digit returns through multiple market cycles, including the 2008 financial crisis and the 2020 pandemic.

Key Takeaways from This Episode

  • Why the "Yale Model" of alternative investing has evolved and what's replaced it
  • The specific private equity strategies yielding 20%+ IRRs in today's market
  • How to access direct lending opportunities with 12-18% annual yields
  • The infrastructure investments that will dominate the next decade
  • Marcus's framework for evaluating hedge fund managers and fee structures
  • Real estate strategies beyond traditional REITs and rental properties
  • How billionaire families structure their alternative investment portfolios

The Alternative Investment Landscape Has Changed

"Most people think alternative investments are just hedge funds and private equity," Marcus explains, "but the real opportunities today are in areas like infrastructure debt, specialty lending, and direct co-investments. The strategies that worked in the 2000s have been arbitraged away by institutional capital."

He reveals how his clients have pivoted away from traditional private equity funds toward direct investments, co-investment opportunities, and specialized credit strategies that institutions can't easily access due to size constraints.

Actionable Investment Framework

Marcus doesn't just share theory—he provides his actual allocation framework that his billionaire clients use to structure their alternative investment portfolios. This includes specific percentage allocations, risk management protocols, and the minimum investment thresholds needed to access institutional-quality opportunities.

He also reveals the three questions he asks every fund manager, the red flags that immediately disqualify investment opportunities, and the specific due diligence process that has helped him avoid every major blow-up in the alternative investment space over the past two decades.
