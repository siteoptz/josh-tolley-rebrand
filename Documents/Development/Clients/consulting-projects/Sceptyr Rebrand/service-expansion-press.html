Sceptyr Launches $500M Expansion Into Emerging Wealth Centers

Following record growth, wealth management firm targets tech entrepreneurs and crypto wealth in Austin, Nashville, and Denver

December 20, 2025 • 4 minute read • For Immediate Release
DALLAS, TX - December 20, 2025 - Sceptyr, LLC

Sceptyr, the rapidly growing wealth management firm specializing in ultra-high-net-worth individuals, today announced a $500 million strategic expansion into three emerging wealth centers: Austin, Texas; Nashville, Tennessee; and Denver, Colorado. The expansion follows a record year of 127% growth in assets under management and positions Sceptyr to capture the explosive growth in technology entrepreneur and cryptocurrency wealth in these markets.

Strategic Market Expansion

The new offices will serve the rapidly expanding population of tech entrepreneurs, cryptocurrency investors, and high-growth business owners who have migrated to these markets over the past five years. Unlike traditional wealth centers like New York and San Francisco, these emerging markets lack institutional-quality wealth management services tailored to digital asset wealth and alternative investment strategies.

"We're following our clients," said Josh Tolley, Founder and CEO of Sceptyr. "The geography of American wealth is shifting dramatically. Austin has become the unofficial capital of crypto wealth, Nashville is attracting entertainment and media moguls, and Denver is emerging as a hub for outdoor recreation and cannabis entrepreneurs. These clients need sophisticated wealth management that understands their unique circumstances."

Traditional wealth management firms are still structured for oil fortunes and old money. We're building for entrepreneurs who sold companies to Google, crypto investors who bought Bitcoin at $100, and business owners whose companies grew 1000% during the digital transformation. These clients need strategies that didn't exist five years ago.
- Josh Tolley, Founder & CEO, Sceptyr

Specialized Service Offerings

Each new office will feature dedicated alternative investment platforms designed specifically for clients with digital asset wealth and non-traditional investment portfolios. Services will include cryptocurrency tax planning, digital asset custody solutions, and access to private equity and venture capital opportunities typically reserved for institutional investors.

The expansion also introduces Sceptyr's new Family Office Lite service for clients with $10-50 million in net worth—a market segment traditionally underserved by both traditional wealth managers and full-service family offices. This service provides institutional-quality investment management, tax planning, and estate planning without the overhead of a complete family office structure.

Technology Integration

All new offices will feature Sceptyr's proprietary wealth management platform, integrating traditional portfolio management with digital asset tracking, alternative investment monitoring, and real-time tax optimization. The platform connects directly to major cryptocurrency exchanges, private equity funds, and alternative investment platforms to provide unified portfolio oversight.

Leadership and Staffing

Sceptyr has recruited senior wealth management professionals with specific experience in emerging wealth demographics for each new location. The Austin office will be led by former Goldman Sachs Private Wealth Management director Sarah Chen, who previously managed over $1.2 billion in technology executive portfolios.

The Nashville office will be directed by Michael Rodriguez, former head of entertainment wealth management at Morgan Stanley, while the Denver office will be led by Jennifer Walsh, a specialist in cannabis industry wealth management and alternative investments.

Hiring and Local Investment

The expansion is expected to create 150 new jobs across the three markets over the next 18 months, including wealth advisors, portfolio managers, tax specialists, and client service professionals. Sceptyr is also investing in local partnerships with accounting firms, law firms, and investment banks to create comprehensive wealth management ecosystems in each market.

Market Opportunity and Growth Projections

Industry research indicates that high-net-worth populations in secondary markets are growing at double the rate of traditional wealth centers. The migration of wealthy individuals to tax-advantaged states, combined with the creation of new wealth in technology and cryptocurrency markets, creates an estimated $150 billion addressable market in Sceptyr's target demographics.

"We're not just expanding geographically—we're expanding demographically," noted Tolley. "The next generation of wealthy Americans looks very different from the previous generation. They're younger, more tech-savvy, and their wealth is structured differently. Traditional wealth management hasn't adapted to serve them effectively."

About Sceptyr

Founded in 2019, Sceptyr specializes in comprehensive wealth management for ultra-high-net-worth individuals and families with complex financial profiles. The firm's approach combines traditional wealth management with expertise in alternative investments, digital assets, and tax optimization strategies. Sceptyr manages over $2.8 billion in client assets and serves more than 400 families across the United States.
