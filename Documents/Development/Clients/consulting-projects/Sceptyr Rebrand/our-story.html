Our Story

Built for those others overlook - a journey of understanding that true wealth transcends net worth

The Vision Behind Sceptyr

Sceptyr was born from a simple observation: while traditional financial institutions chase market cap and net worth, they often overlook the most driven wealth builders—those who understand that exceptional results require exceptional thinking.

We recognized a gap in the market where hardworking individuals and families with vision and commitment were being underserved by cookie-cutter approaches. These are the people who don't need to be convinced that alternative strategies exist; they arrive knowing that traditional advice won't build the legacy they envision.

Our founding principle was clear: partnership should be based on ambition and commitment, not just existing assets. The most successful wealth builders often started with nothing but relentless drive and the intelligence to recognize opportunity.

Our Journey

From inception to becoming a trusted partner for wealth builders nationwide

2008

Foundation

Sceptyr was founded with a revolutionary approach: focus on the potential of driven individuals rather than their current net worth. Our first clients were entrepreneurs and professionals who understood that wealth creation requires more than traditional advice.

2012

Expansion

Reached $500M in assets under management by developing custom wealth strategies for high-net-worth families. Established partnerships with exclusive investment platforms previously available only to institutional investors.

2017

Innovation

Launched our alternative investment platform, providing qualified clients access to hedge funds, private equity, and international opportunities. Crossed $1.5B in structured assets.

2021

Digital Evolution

Integrated cutting-edge technology to provide real-time portfolio management and 24/7 client access to their wealth strategies. Maintained our personal touch while scaling sophisticated services.

2025

Present Day

Managing over $2.4B in assets for 250+ families nationwide. Recognized as a leader in alternative wealth strategies and generational planning, with a 98% client retention rate.

Leadership Team

Experienced professionals dedicated to your financial success

Josh Tolley

Founder & CEO

Josh Tolley started Sceptyr as a stand-alone subsidiary of his company; Tolley & Co after twenty-five years of starting, buying, building, selling companies. He was noticing too many people were being underserved by the established M&A business brokerages. Too little experience, too much focus on getting hundreds of listings, and certainly no backend support to help ensure the success of the clients after they buy or sell a company was too much to ignore.

Sceptyr is an independent company but also part of the Tolley & Co Business Group meaning that the Sceptyr clients are supported by and have access to a number of other companies and resources that can be deployed to assist in buying, selling, and growing your business empire.
