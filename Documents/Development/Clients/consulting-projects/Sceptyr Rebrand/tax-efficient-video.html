About the Instructor: Robert Chen, Former IRS Senior Counsel

Robert Chen spent 18 years at the IRS, including 8 years as Senior Counsel in the Office of Chief Counsel, where he specialized in high-net-worth estate and gift tax matters. He was the lead attorney on cases involving some of America's wealthiest families and helped draft regulations governing advanced estate planning techniques. Robert now serves as Managing Partner at Chen & Associates, advising ultra-high-net-worth families on tax-efficient wealth transfer strategies.

Series Overview

This comprehensive video masterclass reveals the sophisticated legal strategies that billionaire families use to transfer unlimited wealth across generations while paying zero estate taxes. Unlike theoretical academic discussions, this series provides practical, implementable techniques based on actual IRS rulings, court cases, and regulatory guidance.

Robert Chen draws from his extensive experience as the IRS attorney who reviewed and approved many of these strategies, providing unprecedented insight into how these techniques work, why they're legal, and how they can be properly implemented for families with $10+ million in net worth.

What Makes This Series Unique

Most estate planning education focuses on basic techniques available to any high-net-worth family. This masterclass reveals the advanced strategies that only billionaire families and their advisors typically know about—techniques that require sophisticated legal documentation and precise implementation but can save tens or hundreds of millions in estate taxes.

Robert provides actual case studies from his IRS career, showing how families like the Waltons, Gettys, and other billionaire dynasties have used these techniques to transfer billions tax-free. More importantly, he explains the specific legal requirements and potential pitfalls that can invalidate these strategies if not properly implemented.

Inside Access to IRS Perspective

As the former IRS attorney who reviewed many of these transactions, Robert provides unique insight into how the IRS evaluates these strategies, what triggers audits, and how to structure transactions to minimize scrutiny.
